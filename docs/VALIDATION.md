## How to run a session (15–20 minutes)

1. Explain that UdyamPulse is a student prototype on synthetic data, not a lending product, and ask for consent
   to take notes, recording role only.
2. Show the 5-minute guided demo on the live link, or on `make demo` locally.
3. Ask the questions for that person's role, then capture what surprised them and what they would change.
4. Within a day, write one row in the results table and note what the team changed because of it.

## Interview guide

### Banker or credit officer (5 questions)

1. How do you assess a manufacturing MSME today, and which document or signal do you trust least?
2. Looking at the Factory Health Score and its five reasons, would you act on them? What is missing before you
   would?
3. Would the credit memo (with policy citations) save you time? Which section would you rewrite every time?
4. When an early-warning alert arrives 60–90 days before stress, what do you do first, and what would make you
   ignore it?
5. What would your risk and compliance teams need to see before allowing a pilot?

### MSME owner (5 questions)

1. When did you last apply for working capital, and what made it slow or unsuccessful?
2. Would you share GST, bank and electricity data through an Account Aggregator if it got you a faster answer?
   What would worry you?
3. Is the loan-readiness score and its 90-day plan understandable? Try it in Hindi: is anything unclear?
4. Would WhatsApp-style updates from your bank about your business health be welcome or intrusive?
5. If the bank said "no", what would you need to hear to trust that decision?

### Peers or mentors (3 questions)

1. In one sentence, what problem does this solve, and for whom?
2. Which claim did you find least convincing, and why?
3. What would you cut to make the core stronger?
