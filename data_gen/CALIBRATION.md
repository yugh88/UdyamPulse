# Synthetic data calibration

**No real entity data is used anywhere in UdyamPulse.** Every factory, name, identifier and number is
generated by `data_gen/generate.py` from a fixed seed (`UP_SEED=42`). Names are random combinations of
common surnames and generic product words; identifiers use a `SYN` prefix
(e.g. `UDYAM-SYN-0042`) so they cannot collide with real Udyam registrations or GSTINs.
The generator creates **no protected attributes** (gender, religion, caste, etc.).

The *ranges* are informed by public, aggregate statistics only:

| Parameter | Synthetic range | Informed by (public aggregates) |
|---|---|---|
| Udyam category split | 60% micro / 40% small | Udyam registration dashboards; micro units dominate registrations (we over-weight small units so both groups are well represented) |
| Annual turnover | micro ₹1.2–10 Cr, small ₹10–60 Cr | Revised Udyam thresholds (Apr 2025): micro ≤ ₹10 Cr, small ≤ ₹100 Cr |
| 12-month default rate | ~6–9% overall, higher for micro | TransUnion CIBIL–SIDBI *MSME Pulse* reports (MSME NPA / slippage ranges, higher for smaller ticket sizes) |
| Stress-event incidence | ~12% of units over the horizon | Same; SMA-1/SMA-2 incidence in MSME Pulse |
| Seasonality by sector | ±5–14% monthly | GSTN / e-way bill monthly generation statistics (festival and harvest peaks, monsoon dips for ceramics) |
| e-way bill value vs e-invoice value | 86–97% | e-way bills are required for goods movement > ₹50,000; services/B2C excluded |
| Electricity intensity (kWh per ₹1 lakh revenue) | 55 (food) – 160 (ceramics) | Sector energy-intensity benchmarks (BEE MSME cluster studies) |
| Days-to-pay | 35–75 days by sector | MSMED Act 45-day payment norm vs. observed delayed-payment patterns (MSME Samadhaan) |
| Raw-material share of revenue | 45–70% | ASI (Annual Survey of Industries) input-cost ratios by industry group |
| GST filing delay | 4–25% of months late in normal times | GSTR-3B timely-filing statistics |

## Stress mechanics

About 12% of factories receive a hidden **stress event** at a random month (one of `buyer_loss`,
`payment_stretch`, `diversion`). Orders, e-way bills and kWh fall 30–50% over 4–8 weeks, then cash
flow deteriorates (balance drop, bounces), receivable days stretch and GST filings slip.
A fraction of stressed units recover; the rest hit **SMA-2** 3–4 months after onset and default one month later.
The generator simulates a hidden 36-month horizon so every visible month (1..24) has a true
12-month-ahead label; only months 1..24 of signals are exposed to the platform.

## Data-quality noise

- ~1.5% missing months per source table (each source drops independently).
- ~0.5% duplicate re-ingested rows (deduplicated in silver).
- ~3% of factories have a systematic 27–35% GST-vs-bank mismatch (cash sales / other bank accounts) —
  realistic false positives for the diversion rule.
- Gaussian noise on all monetary and volume signals.

## Hero demo factory

`UP-F0001 · Sharma Auto Components, Ludhiana` (small, 14 years vintage, ₹18 Cr turnover) is healthy
through month 20 (Jun 2026). Its top buyer cuts orders in month 21: e-way bill value −42% and kWh −32%
vs the prior 2-month baseline, followed by bounces, falling balances and late GST filings in months 22–23.
